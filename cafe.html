Our story

Started with one roaster and a lot of stubbornness.

In 2004, coffee was still a strange thing to order in Kathmandu — most people wanted tea, and most tourists brought their own instant sachets. We opened anyway, with a single roaster in a room the size of a garage, and beans carried down from the hills in sacks on someone's back.

Two decades on, we still buy directly from the same families who sold us that first harvest. No brokers in between, no beans we haven't tasted ourselves. It's slower than it needs to be. We've decided that's fine.

"We're not trying to be Seattle. We're trying to be the best version of a coffee house in Kathmandu — and that's a different, better goal."
